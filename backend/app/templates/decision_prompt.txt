# V2 Decision Analysis Prompt Template

{persona_prompt}

## Your Task

Analyze the given scenario and provide a structured decision analysis in valid JSON format.

Your analysis MUST include:
1. **Title**: 5-10 words, specific, unique (not generic like "Strategic Scenario Analysis")
2. **Risks**: Array of 3+ risks, each with:
   - `description`: >15 words, specific and concrete (not "General uncertainty")
   - `severity`: one of Low, Medium, High, Critical
   - `likelihood`: one of Low, Medium, High
3. **Alternatives**: Array of 3+ options with:
   - `description`: concise, actionable
   - `action_type`: one of Wait, Pivot, Escalate, Delegate, Invest
4. **Citations**: At least 1 source, format: `[Source: cache:<id> | <url>]`

## Few-Shot Examples

### Example 1: Market Entry Risk
```json
{{
  "title": "International Market Expansion Risk Assessment",
  "summary": "Evaluate risks and alternatives for entering Asian markets",
  "description": "Entry into Asian markets presents regulatory, currency, and competitive challenges requiring careful phasing.",
  "risks": [
    {{"description": "Regulatory compliance delays in China could postpone launch by 6-12 months, impacting market capture.", "severity": "High", "likelihood": "High"}},
    {{"description": "Currency volatility could erode margins by 15-20% without hedging strategy.", "severity": "Medium", "likelihood": "Medium"}}
  ],
  "alternatives": [
    {{"description": "Partner with local distributor to reduce regulatory risk", "action_type": "Invest"}},
    {{"description": "Start with Singapore (lighter regulation) as beachhead", "action_type": "Pivot"}}
  ],
  "source_citations": ["[Source: cache:reg_asia_2025 | https://example.com/asia-regs]"]
}}
```

### Example 2: Technical Debt
```json
{{
  "title": "Legacy System Refactor Timing & Cost Trade-off",
  "summary": "Debt accumulation vs. feature velocity",
  "description": "Technical debt threatens velocity; refactoring costs 3-6 months but enables 2x sprint throughput.",
  "risks": [
    {{"description": "Not refactoring: velocity decline accelerates, 40% regression in release cadence over 18 months.", "severity": "High", "likelihood": "High"}},
    {{"description": "Refactoring now: 3-month feature freeze risks losing market momentum to competitors.", "severity": "High", "likelihood": "Medium"}}
  ],
  "alternatives": [
    {{"description": "Gradual refactor: dedicate 20% team capacity to incremental improvements while shipping features", "action_type": "Pivot"}},
    {{"description": "Defer 6 months, build new parallel system incrementally", "action_type": "Wait"}}
  ],
  "source_citations": ["[Source: cache:tech_debt_2025 | https://example.com/debt-analysis]"]
}}
```
